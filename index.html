02.About Me

Munem Shahriar Nijhum is a Computer Science and Engineering student at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET). He was a former student at Notre Dame College, Dhaka and Kushtia Zilla School, Kushtia.

Technically, he is well-rounded across both software and hardware domains. On the software side, he is proficient in C, C++, Java, Python and JavaScript, with web development experience in HTML, CSS, PHP, ASP.NET and Laravel. His project portfolio is notably diverse — ranging from a Telegram bot that serves academic resources to his entire CSE batch, to a 20-bit mini computer simulated in Logisim, to full Android and desktop food delivery applications built in Java, to a Numerical Calculator developed collaboratively in C++. He has also built personal and club portfolio websites and a Java-based CV builder.

On the hardware side, he has hands-on experience constructing physical circuits — including 7-segment displays, RTL & DTL logic circuits and a relay circuit using Arduino Uno — and has built a controlled autonomous car. His first IoT project involved a Wi-Fi-controlled 7-segment display using an ESP32 and the Blynk platform.

Beyond academics and projects, Munem is actively involved in the tech and science community. He is a member of KUET's competitive programming group (SGIPC) and its software research community (Bit2Byte), and serves as a Performance Analyst and Batch Representative for the Hardware Acceleration Club of KUET. He also volunteered at KUET's national CSE fest, Bitfest 2025, and has been a research member and event host for the Kushtia Science Research Club. Earlier, he was active in science and math clubs at both Notre Dame College and Kushtia Zilla School.

Overall, Nijhum tries to maintain his academic journey with a strong foundation in both theory and practical engineering, a track record of academic distinction and a genuine enthusiasm for building things — from software applications to physical hardware systems.
